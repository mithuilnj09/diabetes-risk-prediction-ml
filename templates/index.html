Problem Statement & Clinical Objective

Understanding the healthcare challenge and predictive machine learning intervention.

“To develop a Machine Learning model that predicts the risk of diabetes using patient health data for early detection and preventive healthcare.”

Diabetes mellitus is one of the most prevalent chronic conditions worldwide, leading to cardiovascular complications, renal failure, retinopathy, and neuropathy if left undetected. Traditional clinical screenings often catch hyperglycemia late in its trajectory.

This system integrates supervised machine learning with verified medical datasets to identify early predictive patterns across non-invasive and routine lab markers (including blood glucose, insulin response, body mass index, blood pressure, and age) to alert individuals and physicians before irreversible metabolic complications arise.

Machine Learning Architecture & Workflow

End-to-end data science and clinical prediction pipeline.

Step 01

Dataset Ingestion & Inspection

Dynamic ingestion of the Kaggle diabetes dataset. Automatic column schema discovery, class distribution profiling, and detection of target outcome variables.

Step 02

Physiological Cleaning & Imputation

Medically impossible zero values (in glucose, diastolic pressure, skinfold, and BMI) are detected and imputed using median statistics on the training partition to prevent data leakage.

Step 03

Multi-Algorithm Benchmark

Rigorous cross-comparison across Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, K-Nearest Neighbors, and Support Vector Machine on balanced validation metrics.

Step 04

Pipeline Serialization & Inference

The top-performing model is packaged into a self-contained Scikit-Learn pipeline via Joblib, enabling instantaneous patient risk scoring with probability outputs.
